About

I'm a Product Manager in training with an unusual head start: two years writing the code that PMs usually just hand off. I spent that time at Accenture on Hilton's property engagement platform, turning messy stakeholder asks into scoped requirements and watching which fixes actually moved reliability for hotel staff on the ground.

Since then I've run seven independent case studies — Porter, Zomato, Twitter/X, Uber, Amazon and Myntra — each one taken from a real observed problem through research, RICE prioritization, a PRD, and (for my flagship project) a working prototype. I like problems with a number attached: a conversion rate, a drop-off point, a retention curve I can move.
